# Crew stays (#207, first slice of #205)

The trip's Stays section (`/trips/:id/stays`, [trip-workspace.md](trip-workspace.md)) contains optional Stays. Organizers can save a name-only draft, add dates and shared details later, and edit/remove stays. Members read the same saved information without organizer actions. A property website opens in a new tab. A stay can also have a confirmed place, found with search or typed as coordinates ([trip-locations.md](trip-locations.md), #208); lodging never changes an activity destination or its weather coordinates.

Check-in includes that night; check-out excludes it. Friday–Sunday is two nights. The previous night's assigned stay supplies a day's Starting from; that day's assigned night supplies Staying tonight. A pre-trip night can explicitly supply the first morning's origin. Undated stays, an unplanned night and No stay needed never supply an origin. The night list includes the itinerary's last night and its optional pre-trip night.

Stay dates and night assignments are separate. Explicit replacement transfers only the reviewed nights, retaining each original stay and its booking status. Editing notes or booking status preserves current assignments. Removal makes its remaining assigned nights unplanned; it does not cancel a reservation. Dates outside the itinerary are preserved and flagged for review. Since #331 each night is planned per person; see below.

## Attendance and per-person nights (#331)

Six people can stay in two places on the same night, arrive late, leave early or move mid-trip, and changing one person's night never changes anyone else's. Migration `20261021201500_trip_attendance_lodging.sql` adds the two tables; the server calculates everything below and the API returns it in `lodging`.

**Attendance.** A person with no saved attendance is on the trip from the pre-trip night through the last night, follows the trip's dates and takes part every day. Otherwise `arrival_date` (pre-trip night to last night) and `departure_date` (first day to the morning after the last night) work like a stay's check-in and check-out: the arrival night is on the trip, the departure night isn't, and they're there on both days. `skipped_dates` are days they're there but not taking part, such as an evening arrival: Casey arrives Saturday, skips Saturday and sleeps in the rental Saturday night. Explicit dates are kept when the trip's dates change and listed in `attendance.review_dates`. Attendance never changes membership, and never deletes a saved kit or a conditions review: a preview lists each affected day and whether a kit stays saved there.

**One state per person and night**, for every night from the pre-trip night through the last night:

- Assigned: a stay, whose dates must include the night.
- No accommodation needed.
- Not planned: no saved row.
- Not on trip: outside their attendance, or they left the trip. It wins over any saved row; rows kept by a trip date change are listed in `review_nights`.

Gaps are Not planned nights between trip days. The pre-trip and last nights are planned only by choice, so they're never gaps. Pending invitations and managed guests keep their membership (`membership`: invited, guest); a pending invitation's nights are kept when it's accepted.

**Whose nights a change sets.** Each change below is one transaction:

- `assign` sets only the listed people and nights. Assigning a stay without `dates` means every night of the stay the person is on the trip.
- Saving a stay without `occupants` keeps the people already staying there: nights newly inside its dates go to them, nights it no longer covers become Not planned, and nights it already covered keep everyone's plan, explicit moves included. A stay nobody is in yet gives its nights to the crew. With `occupants`, exactly those people stay there for all its dates.
- The crew-wide night actions (`PUT …/lodging-nights/:date`) set everyone on the trip that night.
- Attendance clears the person's plans for the nights they're no longer there. New nights start Not planned: nobody is assumed to stay anywhere.

"The crew" here is joined accounts, the organizer included, and managed guests. Pending invitations haven't said they're coming, so they're planned only individually and count in a night's crew summary only once they have a plan for it.

A change that would replace someone's existing plan for a night (another stay, or No accommodation needed for an assigned night), or that attendance would clear, is a conflict. The preview flags each person/night pair in `changes[].conflict`, `conflicts` lists their dates, and the save needs `replace_nights: true`. Clearing a night is never a conflict. Overlapping stays are fine for different people.

**The crew's view.** `nights[]` and `days[]` keep their fields for views that show one plan per night. A split night's `label` names each group ("Pine rental (3) · Village hotel (2)"), `occupancy` lists who is where in stay order, and `stay_id`/`starting_stay_id` are the stay most people are in or start from (ties go to the first saved stay). Day origins count only the people taking part that day, and the crew's drive estimate (#209) starts from where most of them slept. `days[].origins` lists every group; since #333 each one has its own drive ([trip-locations.md](trip-locations.md#departures-333)).

**Migration.** Each crew night is copied to every joined account and managed guest on the trip, with its stay or No stay needed. Pending invitations, and anyone invited later, start Not planned. Stays, their dates, booking status and notes are unchanged. The crew-wide `trip_lodging_nights` is kept, unused, for audit and an API rollback.

**Concurrency.** Every change still needs the reviewed `lodging_revision`, a mutation identity for replay, and the trip lock. Attendance changes bump the revision; membership changes don't touch the trips row (to avoid the lock-order deadlock noted in the shared-gear migration). Instead `lodging.people_key` (and each preview's `people_key`) names who's on the crew. Every save sends the key its review showed as `expected_people_key`; a save without one gets 400. The transaction locks the trip and then its member rows, in the same order as shared gear, so nobody can join, leave or be removed until it commits, and it rejects the save with 409 if the crew differs from the review. A retried save always reaches the transaction, which returns its first result.

## Who's staying where (#332)

The Stays section shows the per-person nights above two ways. Members start on **My stays**; the organizer, and a link to a stay the member isn't in, start on **Everyone**.

- **My stays:** the person's own nights in order, each run of nights in one place as one line ("Pine rental · Fri Oct 9 → Sun Oct 11 · 2 nights", ending on the check-out morning), a line for each morning they leave one stay and sleep in another ("Sun Oct 11: leave Pine rental in the morning, then Mountain lodge that night"), and the stays they're in. The night before the trip and the last night show only when they're planned.
- **Everyone, wide:** people down the side and nights across. Each run of nights in one place is one cell spanning those nights, with a rule across it, so continuity reads without a pill per night. Choosing a cell shows its details beside one vertical rule: the stay's facts, the next morning, and, for the organizer, Change these nights and the person's trip dates. It appears once the workspace is 48rem wide; below that (phones, tablets, a 1024px window with the sidebar open) the stays list carries everyone's nights.
- **Everyone, narrow:** each stay with its nights and named occupants, consecutive nights with the same people together ("Fri Oct 9 → Sat Oct 10 · 1 night: Alex, Blair, Dana"). A pending invitation reads "(invitation pending)".
- **Nights without a stay:** each person's Not planned nights between trip days and their No accommodation needed nights, by person and date. A late arrival's earlier nights are Not on trip, so they aren't listed; nor are a pending invitation's.
- Each stay states its booking, nights and location as separate facts: Booked or Booking not confirmed, Nights not set, and Location set or Location needed for directions.

The server works out the runs (`people[].runs`, with `gap`), the changeovers (`people[].changeovers`), each stay's nights by people (`stays[].occupancy`) and its `checkout_label`; the page only lays them out.

**Editing (organizer, per-person nights only).** A stay's editor starts Name · Nights · Who is staying here?, then location, booking and details; on phones it fills the screen. Its people are named checkboxes with an Everyone on the crew shortcut that leaves out pending invitations. A new stay sends who's in it (the crew while nobody is staying anywhere, otherwise nobody until chosen); an edit sends `occupants` only when the people changed, so a notes or booking edit never moves anyone. Change nights (from a cell, a stay's night line or a gap) sets chosen people, nights and one plan: a stay, No accommodation needed or Not planned. Nights someone isn't on the trip are left out of what's sent. With one person chosen, Not on the trip those nights? switches the same sheet to their trip dates (first night, morning they leave, days there but not taking part). Every preview leads with `summary`, the change in words ("Move Blair and Dana from Pine rental to Village hotel for the night of Sat Oct 10."), says nobody else's nights change, and lists the mornings after. Conflicts name their dates and need the same explicit replacement. Before the migration, `per_member` is false: the page keeps the crew-wide night actions and shows no per-person editing.

## Comparing stays (#213)

**Compare stays** on the Stays page opens `/trips/:id/stays/compare`, a page inside Stays. It puts two or three places side by side by the drives to the days you've planned, so the crew can see the commute tradeoff before choosing.

**Options are separate from stays.** The organizer can save a place they're considering as an option: a name, and optionally its place (the same picker as a stay), address, property link, notes for the crew, the nights it's for and an estimated total cost. An option is only that. Saving, changing or removing one never plans a night, never sets anyone's starting point, never appears in departures, the map or the day brief, and never records a booking ("No booking recorded"). Its nights may overlap any stay's, booked ones included. A trip can have up to 30. Members see options and compare them, without the organizer's Change and Remove.

**What's compared.** Choose two or three, from the saved stays and the options, and the nights: a first night (the pre-trip night through the last night) and a check-out morning. By default the nights are the trip's own, from the first day to the last day's morning. The drives are on the mornings after those nights, check-out morning included and the first night's own morning not, as #209 has it. Each place is tried as everyone's stay those nights (the crew, and anyone else already planned for a night there), and the trip's own origin rules ([trip-locations.md](trip-locations.md#departures-333)) then say who leaves from it each morning:

- A day with **No drive planned**, or its **own starting point**, is the same whichever stay is chosen. It shows once, "the same for every stay", and isn't a drive to compare. So are a day with no destination, access point or exact access point, a day nobody takes part in, and one where everyone has their own starting point.
- A person's own starting point that morning (#333) is noted ("1 person starts from their own starting point that morning, whichever stay is chosen").
- A **repeat visit** to the same access point on another day is noted and counted on each day.

Every value is a one-way drive by car, typical traffic, as on a day ([trip-locations.md](trip-locations.md#drive-times-209)): **available** with its age, **not updated** (more than a week old and checking again failed), **unavailable**, **no road route**, the **organizer's estimate** for the same drive on that day, or the option's own **Location needed** / **Approximate location**.

**Figures, never a score.** Each place gets its longest known drive, how many drives are known ("2 of 3 drives known") and the **Sum of planned outbound drives**: one way, every morning compared, repeat visits counted. It isn't total trip travel and assumes nothing about return drives. Only current figures count: a not-updated, unavailable, no-road-route, missing or approximate drive is shown where it falls, and the place's sum says "Not totaled: 1 drive isn't known" instead of treating it as zero. Sums are compared only when every place's drives are all known over the same mornings: "Ridge chalet has the lowest sum of planned outbound drives, 30 min. Pine rental: 45 min more." Otherwise the summary names what's missing. A morning marks "Shortest that morning" only when every place's drive is known and they differ, and each place's longest drive is marked. There's no "best stay".

**Each place, for these nights.** A saved stay says whether it's chosen for these nights ("Chosen for 2 of these 3 nights · 4 people", or "Nobody is staying here these nights") and its booking. An option says it isn't chosen. Each says when its own nights don't include every night compared, and when it needs a location. The plan for those nights now is listed above, with its booked stays.

**Cost.** An option's cost is the organizer's estimate of the total for the option's nights, in one currency (USD, CAD, EUR, GBP, CHF, AUD, NZD, JPY, NOK or SEK), shown as "About $840 total for Fri Oct 9 → Sun Oct 11 · 2 nights" and labeled an estimate, not a live price. A cost needs the option's nights. One for other nights than those compared says so. Costs in different currencies are never compared or converted, and no cost is ranked.

**Layout.** On a phone (a workspace under 48rem), each morning and each figure is a heading with every place's named value beneath it, so nothing is hidden on another card. Wider, the places are columns of a table, named again in the heading of the cost-and-details table. Long names wrap. There's no map. The page's address keeps what's chosen (`?options=stay:<id>,candidate:<id>&first_night=…&checkout=…`), so a reload keeps it, and a property link opens in a new tab. Adding an option happens in a sheet on the page; a new one joins the comparison while fewer than three are chosen. Comparing never changes the plan: only [Use for these nights](#use-for-these-nights), below, does.

**Routing.** All of it is worked out on the server. A comparison asks only for the chosen places' drives to those mornings' access points, reusing any calculated in the last week for the same pair, from the trip's own estimates or an earlier comparison. The rest are one matrix call per set of winter closures, as for the trip's drives. They're stored in `trip_stay_candidate_routes`, apart from the trip's estimates, whose earlier estimates for a day are shown for review, so an option's drive never reads as one the crew had. The routing credit shows with calculated drives. Each person can compare 30 times a minute.

### Use for these nights

The organizer can choose a compared place for the compared nights with **Use for these nights**, under each place in the comparison. Choosing a column or checkbox alone never changes anything. The sheet opens on a preview from the server, the same one the Stays editors use:

- **Who:** everyone on the trip those nights (the crew, and anyone else already planned for one of them), as named checkboxes. Untick anyone who isn't moving; nights someone isn't on the trip are left alone. Changing who asks for **Review nights** again.
- **What changes:** the change in words ("Move Alex, Blair and Dana from Pine rental to Ridge chalet for the nights of Fri Oct 9 and Sat Oct 10."), that nobody else's nights change, each night's people with their old → new stay, and the mornings after with where each person starts. A night that replaces someone's existing plan needs **Replace their existing plans for …**, as in the Stays editors.
- **Bookings:** nothing is canceled or changed. The booked stays people leave are named ("Pine rental and Village hotel stay booked: nothing is canceled. Change the booking on Stays once you've changed the reservation."). A saved stay keeps its booking status; an option becomes a saved stay for those nights, booking not confirmed.

A saved stay must already include every night: otherwise the sheet says which nights it doesn't, and its dates are changed on Stays first. An option becomes a stay with the option's own id, name, place, address, link and notes (not its cost), for exactly the compared nights, with the chosen people. The comparison then shows it as that stay. The option itself is kept but listed with the stays from then on (it's an option again if that stay is removed), so a retried save can send the same change. Other options stay as they are.

The option is saved only as its preview showed it. The preview returns the option's `candidate_revision`, and the save sends it back as `expected_candidate_revision`: an option edited or removed since then is refused (409, "This option changed since you reviewed it. Review it again.") and nothing is saved. `use_trip_stay_candidate` checks it under the trip lock in the same transaction as the lodging save. Until its migration is applied, the API checks it just before the save instead.

The save carries the previewed revision, crew and a mutation identity through `mutate_trip_lodging`, so a change meanwhile is refused (409) and **Review again** reloads the plan and previews it again, keeping who was chosen. A retried save returns its first result: retrying an option's save after its answer was lost rebuilds the same `save` from the unchanged option, even though the stay now exists, rather than a different change the transaction would refuse as a reused identity.

## Save, access and recovery

The server calculates date coverage, night counts, overlap previews and day origins. Preview precedes Save, with explicit acknowledgement for overlapping nights. The sheet retains entered values after validation/transport failures, focuses the rejected field, announces progress/results and closes after confirmed save. Cancel, Escape and browser Back ask Keep editing / Discard changes when there are edits. A same-URL history entry lets clean Back close the sheet. Reload/closing the tab uses the browser's unsaved-change prompt.

Each mutation includes a client UUID and the lodging revision that was reviewed. `mutate_trip_lodging` locks the trip, rechecks ownership and revision, then commits the stay, assignments, incremented revision and replay receipt together. A stale session receives 409; Review saved plan shows the current snapshot while preserving pending text. Retrying an uncertain response uses the same mutation identity and returns the original result without overwriting newer work. Receipts retain a request hash and result, without a copy of the address or crew notes. The transaction checks for the same property/address/dates before adding a duplicate; later visits with different dates are supported.

All API reads require the existing trip membership/owner checks; writes require the owner. Cross-trip stay IDs are rejected in the transaction and by the composite foreign key. New tables have RLS enabled with no browser policies or grants, and the RPC is executable only by `service_role`, matching migration 014 and the server-only Supabase client.

## API integration

`GET /api/v1/trips/:id` includes `lodging` with its revision, `people_key`, `per_member`, stay summaries (with `occupants`), saved `assignments` and `attendance`, readable crew `nights` and derived day contexts, and `people`: each person's membership, attendance, nights, days (participation, starting point, tonight's stay) and gaps. Existing trips return empty stays and unplanned nights. Before the migration, `per_member` is false and the nights are the crew's.

- `POST /api/v1/trips/:id/stays/preview`: `{action: "save" | "remove" | "night" | "assign" | "attendance", expected_revision, expected_people_key?, stay?, existing?, occupants?, stay_id?, date?, status?, assignments?, member_id?, attendance?}`. No write occurs. Returns the crew's `nights` before and after, each person/night in `changes` (before, after, the next morning and that person's origin then, and whether it's a conflict), `summary` (the changes in words, one sentence for each set of people who change the same way; empty before per-person nights), `conflicts` by date, attendance's `day_changes`, and dates outside the itinerary.
- `POST /api/v1/trips/:id/stays`: `{stay, occupants?, expected_revision, expected_people_key, mutation_id, replace_nights?}`. A stay has a stable UUID, required name, paired nullable `check_in`/`check_out`, and optional type/address/property URL/local times/shared notes. Booking status defaults to `not_booked` and requires explicit `booked` selection.
- `PATCH /api/v1/trips/:id/stays/:stayId`: same save contract, with the stay identity supplied by the route.
- `DELETE /api/v1/trips/:id/stays/:stayId`: `{expected_revision, expected_people_key, mutation_id}`.
- `PUT /api/v1/trips/:id/lodging-nights/:date`: `{status: "no_stay" | "unplanned", expected_revision, expected_people_key, mutation_id, replace_nights?}`, for everyone on the trip that night.
- `PUT /api/v1/trips/:id/lodging-assignments` (#331): `{assignments: [{member_id, status: "assigned" | "no_stay" | "unplanned", stay_id?, dates?}], expected_revision, expected_people_key, mutation_id, replace_nights?}`. Up to 200 entries and 2,000 person/nights, a whole stay's nights included; a night listed twice is rejected.
- `PUT /api/v1/trips/:id/members/:memberId/attendance` (#331): `{attendance: {arrival_date, departure_date, skipped_dates}, expected_revision, expected_people_key, mutation_id, replace_nights?}`. Nulls and an empty list mean the whole trip.

Only the organizer previews or saves any of these; members read the plan, and outsiders and people who left get 404. Before the migration the two per-person routes, `occupants` and per-person previews return 503 without calling the transaction, and crew-wide changes work as before.

Stay options and comparisons (#213):

- `GET /api/v1/trips/:id/stay-candidates`: `{available, candidates, first_nights, checkouts, default_window}`. Each option has its fields, `revision`, `date_label`, `cost_label` and `review_dates` (nights the trip no longer includes). `available` is false before the migration. Anyone on the trip.
- `POST /api/v1/trips/:id/stay-candidates`: `{candidate, expected_revision: null}`, organizer only. An option has a client UUID, a name (1–200 characters), paired nullable `check_in`/`check_out`, optional `address`, `property_url` (http or https), `notes` (2,000 characters) and `location`, and `cost_amount` (0 to 10,000,000, two decimals) with `cost_currency`, which need the option's nights. 400 with `field` for each.
- `PATCH /api/v1/trips/:id/stay-candidates/:candidateId`: `{candidate, expected_revision}`; `DELETE`: `{expected_revision}`. Both organizer only.
- `GET /api/v1/trips/:id/stay-candidates/compare?options=stay:<id>,candidate:<id>[,…]&first_night=&checkout=`: `TripStayComparison`, the window and the plan for its nights now, each place's facts and figures, each morning's drives in the order chosen, `summary`, `comparable`, `cost_note`, `closures` and `credit`. 400 with `field` for one or four places, a repeat or nights outside the trip; 404 for a stay or option that isn't the trip's; 429 past 30 a minute. Anyone on the trip.
- `POST /api/v1/trips/:id/stay-candidates/use`: `{option, first_night, checkout, member_ids?, preview: true}` returns the lodging preview with `member_ids`, `stay_name`, `new_stay` and `booked_kept`; `{…, preview: false, member_ids, expected_revision, expected_people_key, mutation_id, replace_nights?, expected_candidate_revision?}` saves it (`expected_candidate_revision` is the preview's `candidate_revision`, required for a new stay from an option), as Change nights (`assign`) for a saved stay or Add stay with `occupants` (`save`) for an option. Organizer only; 400 with `field` for nights outside a stay's dates or nobody chosen, 404 for a stay, option or person that isn't the trip's, 409 for a changed plan, 503 before per-person nights.

Saves go through `mutate_trip_stay_candidate`, which locks the trip, checks the organizer and compares the revision the editor opened. A retried save that finds the same fields saved, or a retried removal that finds it gone, returns that. Any other mismatch changes nothing: 409 with the option as it's saved now (`current`), which the editor shows while keeping what was typed, and saving again replaces it. 404 when it was removed meanwhile (the editor keeps the entries to save as a new one), 409 for the same name, address and nights already saved as an option, 503 before the migration.

A trip date change (#176, [trip-editing.md](trip-editing.md)) is reviewed first: trip `PATCH` with `preview: true` returns `lodging_after` with the day-plan changes. Saving with existing lodging requires the reviewed `lodging_revision`; otherwise 409 returns the current review. A database trigger increments the lodging revision when trip dates change, invalidating old stay previews. Stay dates, assignments and bookings remain intact.

## Deployment and verification

Using an option for the nights (#213): apply `supabase/migrations/20261117090000_trip_use_stay_candidate.sql` by hand, on its own, after `20261021201500` and `20261116200000`, before or after deploying the API. It adds `use_trip_stay_candidate`, for the service role only. Until it's applied the API checks the option's revision just before the lodging save, outside its transaction. Check afterwards that `SELECT to_regprocedure('public.use_trip_stay_candidate(uuid, text, integer, uuid, jsonb, boolean, uuid, integer)') IS NOT NULL` is true.

Stay options (#213): apply `supabase/migrations/20261116200000_trip_stay_candidates.sql` by hand, on its own, after 017, 025 and 028, before or after deploying the API. It adds `trip_stay_candidates`, `trip_stay_candidate_routes` and `mutate_trip_stay_candidate`, all for the service role only, and changes nothing else. Until it's applied the page says options can't be added yet, saving one returns 503, and saved stays can still be compared, calculating their drives each time. Check afterwards that `SELECT to_regclass('public.trip_stay_candidates') IS NOT NULL AND to_regprocedure('public.mutate_trip_stay_candidate(uuid, text, text, jsonb)') IS NOT NULL` is true.

Per-person nights (#331): deploy the API first, then apply `supabase/migrations/20261021201500_trip_attendance_lodging.sql` individually in the SQL editor. The API reads the crew-wide nights until the new tables exist, so the order only matters for saves. After applying, `SELECT count(*) FROM trip_member_lodging_nights` should equal `SELECT count(*) FROM trip_lodging_nights n JOIN trip_members m ON m.trip_id = n.trip_id AND m.status IN ('joined', 'guest')`.

Apply `supabase/migrations/017_trip_stays.sql` after the existing migrations and before deploying this API. The app does not apply migrations automatically. Production's migration history differs from the local numbered files, so apply this migration individually rather than pushing the whole folder. Missing stay tables cause a retryable trip-load error rather than silently hiding saved accommodation.

Focused tests cover Postgres rollback/replay, duplicate checks, two-session revision conflicts, exact Friday–Sunday coverage, hotel changes, pre-trip nights, overlap replacement, No stay needed, removal, shortened trips, privileges, membership/cross-trip access, invalid/safe URLs, failed saves, retained input, focus and browser Back. New browser requests time out after 20 seconds and retain the save identity for retry.

Local browser checks use labeled fixture data at 320px, 390px and desktop sizes. Production acceptance still requires migration rollout and signed-in organizer/member testing, real mobile keyboard/safe-area behavior, 200% browser zoom, and screen-reader/device verification.
